Content Organization, UX Design, UI Design and Usability Testing

Scroll to Explore

Moubina is a real estate developer based in Agadir. Its website introduces the group and showcases its residential and commercial projects. Visitors can explore project details, browse galleries and floor plans, download brochures, and contact the relevant team for property inquiries or after-sales support.

View case study

My Role

My contributions and responsibilities in this project

UX UI Designer

Key Responsibilities

  • Organized client-provided content into clear page sections and layouts.
  • Organized client-provided content around users’ needs and browsing priorities.
  • Structured navigation to guide visitors from discovering projects to exploring details and making inquiries.
  • Established a visual hierarchy for headings, descriptions, and key information.
  • Balanced imagery, typography, and spacing to keep content readable and engaging.
  • Used consistent UI elements to make interactions familiar and predictable.
  • Gave calls to action clear placement to help users take the next step.
  • Adapted each project’s visual identity to its own logo, while maintaining consistent navigation and usability across project detail pages.

Project Impact

  • Created clear paths from project discovery to property inquiries.
  • Established a consistent visual language balancing brand identity with usability.
  • Supported informed decisions with organized galleries, floor plans, and property details.
  • Helped visitors identify the right contact channel for their needs.
